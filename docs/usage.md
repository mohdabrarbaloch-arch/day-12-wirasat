# Usage Guide

## Quick start

1. **Create an account** (Register tab) or log in.
2. **Enter an estate value** (optional) in Pakistani Rupees — leave 0 for
   fraction-only results.
3. **Select the deceased's heirs** by tapping the cards. For multiple heirs
   of the same type (e.g. 2 daughters), tap **+** to increase the count.
4. Hit **Calculate Shares**.

The result card shows, for every heir:

- the **exact fraction** (e.g. `7/12`) and percentage,
- the **amount in rupees** (when an estate value was entered),
- whether the heir received a fixed Quranic share or the **asabah**
  (residuary) share.

A badge next to the title shows the distribution mode:

| Mode     | Meaning                                                       |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| `normal` | Fixed shares + asabah fit the estate exactly                  |
| `awl`    | Shares exceeded 100% → all reduced proportionally             |
| `radd`   | No residuary heir → surplus returned to the sharers           |

Notes under the badge explain any exclusions (e.g. *"Full Brother excluded —
blocked by a closer heir"*).

## Example

**Deceased:** male, estate Rs 1,200,000. **Heirs:** wife, son, daughter.

| Heir     | Share  | Amount        |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Son      | 7/12   | Rs 700,000.00 |
| Daughter | 7/24   | Rs 350,000.00 |
| Wife     | 1/8    | Rs 150,000.00 |

## Print / share

Tap **Print / Share** to open a clean, formatted distribution sheet you can
print or save as PDF.

## History

Tap the 📜 icon to see your saved calculations. Tap any item to re-open it.

## Important notes

- The calculator implements the **classical Sunni (Hanafi-method)** rules.
- Always confirm a real estate distribution with a **qualified scholar** or
  the local court — this tool is an aid, not a fatwa.
